# circuit-center-alignment-v1

`align_circuit_centers(target, source, max_work=2000000, seconds=30)` is an exact sufficient branch for complete, equally sized 4..7 labelled rational planar centre sets. Both affine ranks must be two. Labels are strict 64-bit integers, distinct coordinates have reduced numerator/denominator at most 256 bits, and bool/float are rejected. Source must contain at least one four-point circuit whose every triple is noncollinear. This is centre alignment only; no whole-block containment, futlyar, or original fits is asserted.

For each of all n! canonical bijections, enumerate all canonical source circuits. Their signed minors give c with c0=1, sum c=0, sum c*y=0 and all coefficients nonzero. Put S=sum abs(c) and v=-sum c*x_pi/S. Every affine map has residuals r_i=T(y_i)-x_pi(i), and sum c*r=S*v. Triangle inequality therefore gives max Euclidean norm(r_i)>=norm(v). The producer records the first circuit maximizing squared norm(v), keeping permutation, circuit indices, vector, and lower_bound_squared. E is the minimum of these recorded bounds. The independent verifier requires each recorded circuit to give a valid bound, but deliberately does not prove that it maximizes the circuits: any valid bound per bijection suffices for the global lower-bound proof.

For a recorded minimum, the producer sets the four circuit residuals to sign(c_i)*v. The first three source points determine the unique affine T; the fourth is affine-consistent by the relation. Success requires invertible T and all n actual residuals equal alpha_i*v with abs(alpha_i)<=1. When v=0, every residual must be zero and every stored alpha is zero. Thus a feasible invertible map attains E and the full ledger proves global optimality across every bijection and every invertible affine map.

Stability additionally requires every ledger entry at E to have vector v or -v. Under a common invertible affine H, relations persist and every stored lower bound becomes H*v_j. The attaining map is conjugated by H; all its residuals remain alpha_i*H*v. Set epsilon=min(1/4, each positive gap/[8*(E_j+E)]). If each entry of H-I is smaller than epsilon then its spectral norm is at most 2epsilon, and the energy change is at most 5epsilon*E_j. Each positive gap therefore persists; parallel equality vectors stay equal. This proves common-affine global persistence. Translation cancels.

For the point-displacement bridge choose the first noncollinear target triple, with difference matrix D. Set K=max column absolute sum of D inverse and epsilon_point_displacement=epsilon/[4*max(1,K)]. Point displacements below this radius imply each entry of H-I is below epsilon, by cancellation on the two difference columns. epsilon1=Fraction(1) is a fixed positive local source-transformation radius: the global result supplies local optimality throughout that radius before and after qualifying common perturbations.

Only success has status GLOBAL_OPTIMAL_STABLE_SUFFICIENT and a certificate. Failed sufficient conditions return UNKNOWN, without claiming that the general optimum is unattained or that general fits do not exist. Invalid domain or absent source circuit returns UNSUPPORTED. Budget exhaustion returns UNKNOWN with partial permutations_completed, circuits_evaluated and candidates_checked counts, and no incomplete success certificate.

`assess_circuit_alignment_certificate` independently checks strict types, complete input binding, exactly n! canonically ordered unique permutations, each legal circuit and exact bound, minimum comparisons, equality directions, invertibility, all n affine substitutions and alpha constraints, and stability radii. It calls neither producer nor affine solver. Stored rational values are bounded at 16384 bits, tuple length at 5040, and nesting depth at 32. Construction and internal independent verification share one cooperative Budget. Public verification has its own budget. These are cooperative accounting/time checks rather than hard process interrupts. Verification returns VERIFIED with classification, INVALID for malformed certificates, or UNKNOWN on budget exhaustion. Existing exact_center.encode_exact supports serializing the exact tuple/Fraction tree, but its decoder has a separate 20000-node bound: larger ledger serialization must use bounded per-row chunks instead of assuming whole-certificate decoding is supported.
